# Deccan Rail Passenger Guide (fictional, for this book)

## Children
Children under 5 travel free and do not need a ticket, but they do not get
their own seat. Children aged 5 to 11 pay half the adult fare.

## Senior citizens
Passengers aged 60 or over receive a 40 per cent discount on the adult
fare. Proof of age must be shown when boarding.

## Luggage
Each passenger may carry two bags free of charge, with a combined weight of
up to 35 kg. Each additional bag costs Rs 150. Bicycles travel only in the
luggage van and cost Rs 300.

## Cancellations and refunds
Tickets cancelled more than 48 hours before departure are refunded in full,
minus a fee of Rs 50. Tickets cancelled between 48 and 4 hours before
departure receive a 50 per cent refund. No refund is given less than
4 hours before departure.

## Pets
Small pets in a closed carrier may travel free on day trains. Pets are not
allowed on overnight trains.

## Window seats
A window seat can be reserved for Rs 40 extra at the time of booking.
Window seats cannot be added after a ticket has been issued.
